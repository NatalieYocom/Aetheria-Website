Privacy Policy

Last updated November 1, 2026

This website

This site has no accounts, no cookies, and no analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address. Whoever hosts this site may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and page requests.

Discord and GitHub

If you join our Discord or visit our GitHub, those services collect data under their own privacy policies. In our Discord, we can see what other members see, such as your username and messages, plus any moderation records we keep.

The game

Aetheria has not been publicly released. Before release, we will update this policy to say what the game collects, such as accounts, voice chat, and uploaded content.

Mailing list

If we add a mailing list, we will use your email only to send Aetheria updates, and you will be able to unsubscribe at any time.

Children

Aetheria is not for children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect their information. If you think we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, on our Discord.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
